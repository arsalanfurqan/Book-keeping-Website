ROUND 1
Seen in one second: the accounting statement in deep teal.
Idea: the brief's clear-accounting promise becomes a large, uninterrupted typographic statement.
Live thing: accounting-priority selector leads to the relevant service and preselected enquiry.
Layout words by section: hero=statement / services=grid / finder=interactive-split / process=list / industries=photographic-grid / payroll=split / faq=accordion / close=statement.
Scores: design 6.5 · usability 8 · creativity 6 · content 7 → overall 6.9.
Caps applied: category-level identity; company-specific evidence is not available.
Top three problems:
1. All text appears too heavy in desktop/mobile screenshots: replace static heavy font downloads with genuine variable Manrope/Inter files.
2. Phone hero contains a long vacant area: reduce mobile minimum height to 630px.
3. Coffee imagery is a weak connection to e-commerce: move that atmospheric photograph to freelancers and use the laptop image for online businesses.

Warnings: the brief fixes Inter as body type, so the font warning is intentional. No real company footage exists; unrelated decorative video would not improve this accounting-services brief. The typography uses multiple responsive sizes across display, heading, lead, body and small roles.
