# FAQ Drafts and Legal Content Checklist

## Suggested FAQ
**What services do you provide?**  
We offer support across bookkeeping, payroll administration, reporting and other agreed accounting needs. Exact scope is confirmed during consultation.

**Can you handle payroll for a growing team?**  
We can discuss recurring payroll support, reporting and administrative needs. Availability of statutory calculation and filings depends on your country and our engagement scope.

**How much does it cost?**  
Pricing depends on business size, transaction volume, number of employees and required services. Request a custom quote.

**What information do you need to get started?**  
We'll begin with basic business information and then explain secure onboarding requirements before requesting sensitive records.

**Do you support online stores?**  
E-commerce bookkeeping and settlement reconciliation can be included when the relevant platforms and data are supported.

**Do you file taxes?**  
Tax preparation or filing availability depends on location and applicable qualifications. Ask us about your situation.

**How quickly can we begin?**  
An onboarding timeline is agreed after reviewing your needs and available records.

## Legal pages required before release
### Privacy Policy (legal counsel must draft or approve)
- Identity of the operator and privacy contact.
- Categories of information collected through forms, analytics and booking tools.
- Purposes and lawful basis/consent by applicable jurisdiction.
- Data recipients (hosting, CRM, email, booking provider).
- International transfers where applicable.
- Retention periods, rights requests and deletion/contact mechanisms.
- Security summary and policy update date.

### Terms of Website Use
- Informational nature of public website content.
- No professional-client relationship established by simply browsing/contacting.
- Intellectual property, acceptable use, liability wording and jurisdiction, as legally approved.

### Professional services disclaimer
Website content is general information, not a personalized accounting, tax or legal opinion. Professional advice and obligations depend on a signed engagement and applicable laws. Obtain legal review of exact wording.

### Cookie notice
Only if relevant to selected analytics/ad tools and target markets; implement actual technical preferences, not cosmetic controls.
