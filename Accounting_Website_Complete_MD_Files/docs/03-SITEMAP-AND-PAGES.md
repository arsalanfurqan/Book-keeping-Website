## Homepage sections in order
1. Header and above-fold hero: clear headline, benefit sentence and two CTAs.
2. Trust proof: real logos/reviews/credentials **only if verified**; otherwise omit.
3. Services: 6 visible cards with View all services link.
4. Why choose us: accuracy, clarity, timely communication, tailored support.
5. Three-step process: discuss, tailor plan, ongoing support.
6. Industry cards: SMEs, e-commerce, startups, freelancers.
7. Short spotlight on payroll, with direct link to payroll page.
8. Verified testimonials/case studies (conditional).
9. FAQ accordion (5–7 questions).
10. Final booking CTA, footer.

## Service listing page
H1 + short introduction; category-filter optional (P1); cards with name, outcomes and links; bottom enquiry CTA.

## Standard service detail template
- Breadcrumb > service name.
- H1 promise without guarantees; 1-paragraph explanation; Book Consultation CTA.
- Who this is for.
- What is included / deliverables.
- How the engagement works.
- What we need from you (high-level).
- Frequently asked questions.
- Related services and final CTA.

## Payroll detail additions
- Payroll administration scope: employee data collection **through secure onboarding, not public form**, pay schedules, payroll preparation, payslips, deductions, employer reports and year-end support **as applicable to country**.
- Distinguish services offered directly from services handled by partners.
- Mention regulatory applicability and specific jurisdictions only after legal validation.
- Lead enquiry optional fields: employee-count range and pay frequency.

## About page
Company story, mission, team and credentials **only when factual**, service approach and CTA.

## Industries page
Each card links to relevant service; include industries served without invented client claims.

## Contact / booking
Form, work email, office/service region, optional WhatsApp and scheduling integration. Display business hours only after confirmation. Success page prevents duplicate submission confusion.

## Per-page SEO fields
Each indexed page needs unique title (aim ~50–60 chars), meta description (~140–160 chars), canonical URL, single H1, internal links, social preview and structured data only if it matches page facts.
